Frequently asked questions

Can I select more than one candidate for a position?

No. Each position allows one candidate only. Selecting another candidate replaces your previous selection for that position.

Can I change my choices before submitting?

Yes. You can change your choices freely before confirming the final ballot.

Is this a real election platform?

No. This is a front-end academic prototype using sample candidates and browser storage. It is not suitable for real elections without a secure backend, authentication, authorization, audit controls, and independent security testing.
